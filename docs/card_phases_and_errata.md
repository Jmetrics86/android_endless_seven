## II. Turn Structure & Phase Sequence

Endless Seven operates on a 3-phase round loop:
1. **Prep Phase**: Draw cards, reinforce lanes face-down, discard remaining cards to Limbo.
2. **Seal Resolution Phase**: Sequential resolution of Sacred Seals 1 through 7 through Steps 0, A, B, C, D, and E.
3. **Cleanup Phase**: Zero-power cleanup, delayed combat sacrifices, influence tallies, and round reset.

---

## III. Phase 1: Prep Phase (Start of Round)

At the start of each round, players draw new cards, place cards face-down into lane slots, and discard unplayed cards to Limbo.

### Limbo Final Act Abilities
Cards discarded to the Limbo zone may trigger **Final Act** abilities during active turn windows or from the Abilities Drawer by paying the cost of moving from Limbo to the Graveyard:
*   **Kaelarion** (Vampyre - 4 PV): While in Limbo, move this card to Graveyard to place a Champion on top of its owner's deck.
*   **Alistar Elren** (Daemon - 6 PV): While in Limbo, move this card to Graveyard to place a -3 Weakness Marker on any creature in play.
*   **Golgothane** (Graveborn - 9 PV): While in Limbo, move this card to Graveyard and shuffle all creatures in your enemy's Limbo back into their deck.
*   **Coal** (Oathbringer - 10 PV): While in Limbo, move this card to Graveyard to stop a creature from Championing a Seal in Step E.
*   **Tarkidos** (Oathbringer - 9 PV): While in Limbo, move this card to Graveyard to Purify an undefended Seal without a Champion.

## IV. Phase 2: Seal Phase (Sequential Seals 1 to 7)

Each of the seven Sacred Seals resolves sequentially through six distinct steps:

### Step 0: Haste Strike
Before cards are flipped face-up or standard combat begins, any card with the **Haste** trait strikes immediately against the defender in its lane.

#### Rules & Mechanics:
1. **Physical Reveal**: The defending card is turned face-up and marked `faceUp = true` so its stats and art are visible.
2. **Haste Damage Priority**: If the defender is destroyed during Step 0, it is sent to the Graveyard. Its **Flip** ability is bypassed and never triggers.
3. **Survivor Preservation**: If the defending card survives the Haste Strike, its initial face-down status (`pWasFaceDown` / `eWasFaceDown`) is retained so its Flip ability can still execute when Step B is reached.
4. **Non-Battler Stymie**: If the defender has `cannotBattleOrBeBattled` (e.g. Cyprian) or `cannotBattleWhilePV1` (Oriel at PV 1), combat cannot initiate and Step 0 concludes without combat damage.

#### Active Cards in Step 0:
*   **Fenris Lightfoot** (Lycan - 1 PV): Has Haste. Battles immediately. Any card battling Fenris is destroyed at end of round (Cleanup).
*   **Lucian Blackwood** (Lycan - 7 PV): Has Haste. Battles immediately. Places +2 Power Marker on self after destroying an enemy creature in battle.
*   **Noble the Great** (Oathbringer - 9 PV): Has Haste. Battles immediately. After destroying an enemy creature in battle, you may destroy another creature or Marker type in play.
*   **Valerius Nightshade** (Vampyre - 2 PV): Has Haste. Battles immediately. Steals 1 Power from opponent before battle. Defender's Flip ability is Nullified.

---

### Step A: The Flip & Tie Rule
Reveal all remaining face-down cards on the active Seal lane.

#### Rules & Mechanics:
1. **Simultaneous Reveal**: All face-down lane cards are rotated face-up.
2. **Step A Tie Rule**: If both newly revealed cards have equal effective power upon reveal (Base Power + Power Markers - Weakness Markers), **both combatants are destroyed immediately** and the Seal becomes/remains Neutral. No Flip or Activate abilities trigger for either card.
3. **Zero-Power Destruction**: Any card whose effective power is <= 0 is immediately destroyed and sent to the Graveyard before Step B.

---

### Step B: Abilities (Flip and Activate)

Abilities are processed in descending order of effective power. If cards have equal power, tie-breaker ordering applies. Cards with `hasNullify: true` (Remiel, Samyaza) execute with highest priority to suppress enemy Flip abilities before they trigger.

### Step C: Alignment Influence & Ward Markers

The victorious or uncontested lane side influences the Seal alignment:
*   **Purify**: Light aligns the Seal.
*   **Corrupt**: Darkness aligns the Seal.
*   **Ward Marker Protection**: If a Seal has a Ward Marker (placed by Anakim the Wise), its alignment cannot be changed by ability or siege, and Champions cannot ascend to it until the Ward Marker expires at end of round.
*   **Luna Reaction**: If an enemy ability or siege attempts to alter the influence of a Seal, Luna may react: move to the Graveyard to nullify the influence change entirely, or hold.
*   **Ability Defender Removal Rule**: If an ability destroys, exiles, or moves an opponent defender to Graveyard or Limbo, that player claims the Seal alignment during Step C, even if their lane slot is empty. Bounce to hand or deck does NOT grant alignment influence.

---

### Step D: Battle Phase & Combat Resolution

Standard combat occurs between surviving lane combatants.

#### Step Bonuses (`battleStepBonusPower`):
*   **Jophiel**: +3 Power during Battle step (Effective power: 4 + 3 = 7).
*   **Belphegor**: +2 Power during Battle step (Effective power: 4 + 2 = 6).
*   **Tarkidos**: +3 Power during Battle step (Effective power: 9 + 3 = 12).

#### Combat Rules:
1. **Effective Battle Power**: `Base Power + Power Markers - Weakness Markers + battleStepBonusPower`.
2. **Combat Invulnerability**: Cards protected by Flip invulnerability (Anakim the Wise, Mammon, Ulfric Thorne, Umbarax) cannot be destroyed by battle damage.
3. **Tied Combat Mutual Destruction**: If both combatants have equal effective battle power (`powA === powB`), **both combatants are destroyed simultaneously** unless protected by invulnerability.
4. **Special Combat Effects**:
    *   **Sulvian Vane** (Vampyre - 5 PV): Any creature that battles Sulvian Vane is placed on top of its owner's deck after combat, regardless of outcome.
    *   **Valerius Nightshade** (Vampyre - 2 PV): Steals 1 Power from opponent before battle damage calculation.
    *   **Noble the Great** (Oathbringer - 9 PV): After destroying an enemy creature in battle, you may destroy another creature or Marker type in play.
    *   **Lucian Blackwood** (Lycan - 7 PV): Place a +2 Power Marker on this creature after destroying an enemy creature in battle.
    *   **Umbarax** (Graveborn - 9 PV): After destroying an enemy creature in battle, place +2 Power Marker + 2 per Graveborn in play on this creature.

---

### Step E: Ascension

If the lane is uncontested or the defender was defeated, and the victorious card is a **Champion**, it ascends to occupy the Seal as its Champion.
*   **Coal Limbo Block**: While Coal is in Limbo, move Coal to the Graveyard to cancel and prevent an enemy creature from Championing the Seal.
*   **Ward Marker Block**: A Seal with a Ward Marker cannot be occupied by a Champion until the Ward Marker is removed or expires.

---

## V. Phase 3: Cleanup Phase (End of Round)

At the end of the round, delayed combat and end-of-turn effects resolve:
1. **Cyprian Self-Sacrifice**: Cyprian is sacrificed and sent to the Graveyard.
2. **Fenris Lightfoot Delayed Destruction**: Any creature that battled Fenris Lightfoot during Step 0 or Step D is destroyed and sent to the Graveyard.
3. **Ward Marker Expiration**: All temporary Ward Markers placed during the round expire.
4. **Status & Ability Reset**: Stored activate flags reset; board-presence power markers re-synchronize.
