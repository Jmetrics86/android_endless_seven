The Endless Seven is a high-stakes dark fantasy saga where the afterlife
is not a place of rest, but a battlefield for the fate of existence. During
the Septennial Reckoning, the barrier between the mortal realm and the
void thins, and seven supernatural points known as the Sacred Seals
unveil themselves. These seals act as a cosmic dam; if they remain pure,
life continues with the rule of law and benevolence. Should they be
corrupted, the dam breaks, and the realm is consumed by an era of
absolute anarchy and discord.

The Phases
I. The Prep Phase (Start of Round)
I. Draw: Take 8 cards.
2. Reinforce: Place cards face-down on vacant Battlefield positions.
3. Purge: Discard remaining hand to Limbo.
2. The Seal Phase (Sequential I - 7)
Resolve each Seal through this loop:
I. The Flip: Reveal cards. Higher Power triggers "Flip" abilities first.
2. The Tie: Equal Power = both cards are destroyed immediately.
Activation Window I (Pre-Battle): Use "Activate" abilities or Paradigms.
Higher Power has priority.
3. Battle: Compare Power, Loser goes to Limbo.
If the Battlefield is clear, you may battle the Champion on the Seal.
Activation Window II (Post-Battle): Use "Activate" abilities or Paradigms.
4. Claim/Ascend: If you killed the defender (via combat or ability destruction/exile to Graveyard/Limbo) or the Seal was empty, flip the Seal to your Alignment, even if you do not have a card present in that lane slot.
   * NOTE (Bounce Exception): Using an ability that sends an opponent defender back to their Hand or to the top/bottom of their Deck does NOT trigger this Seal alignment change if your lane slot is empty.
   If your unit is a Champion, move it from the Battlefield onto the Seal.
3. The Cleanup Phase (End of Round)
I. Duplicate Rule: Sacrifice copies; keep only one of any specific card
name.
2. Persistence: Face-up cards stay on the Battlefield for the next round.
3. Victory Check: If a player controls all 7 Seals, they win instantly.
Otherwise, proceed to the next Round (Max 3).

Winning the Game
I. The Instant Win: The Grand Convergence
The ultimate achievement is to align all seven Seals.
If all 7 are Purified, the cycle of death and rebirth halts, and a permanent utopia is
established. If all 7 are Corrupted, the physical world is consumed by Limbo. The
"New Era" begins instantly. The game ends the moment the 7th Seal flips.
2. The Victory by Tally: The Fragile Balance
If neither side can achieve total dominance after 3 Rounds, the universe enters a
Stagnant Age. The player controlling the majority (4 or more) of the Seals dictates
the favor of the next era, representative of a tactical victory.
3. Victory by Attrition: The Empty Void
If at the start of any Prep Phase, a player is unable to draw the required 8 cards
from their deck, that player loses the game.
4. The Toll of War
In the event of a tie, the player controlling the most Seals with Champions, is
declared the winner. If a tie persists, both players enter a fourth round; the first
player to claim a Seal using a Champion wins the game immediately.
5. Paradigms
The influences, A player may win the game by way of specific conditions met as
described on your Factions Paradigm.

How to Play Duels
The round begins at the first seal and ends at the seventh seal. The order of
operations for each seal is as follows:
Flip: Both teams simultaneously flip over any face-down cards on the current seal,
facing off against each other they Resolve any "Flip" abilities.
Battle: Creatures on the current seal enter battle. Compare their Power values,
factoring in modifiers or
any
abilities. The creature with the higher Power value wins
and remains on the seal. The losing creature is destroyed and placed into limbo.
Resolve any abilities that pertain to the battle or are activated by being destroyed.
After the battle, resolve any abilities of creatures that remain on the field of play.
The two players that won there battles now face off against each other to determine a
winner. In the event of a one battle stalemate the outcome of the other battle takes
The position or the seal. In the event of two stalemates, both teams move to the next
battle. If two players on the same team are victorious in battle, they will then choose
amongst
themselves which creature will remain to occupy the position or the seal.
When a players creature ability destroys an opponent creature in Play, you may Purify
or corrupt a seal. When a player's creature attacks an empty seal, you may purify or
corrupt that seal based on your alignment and then take control of that seal.
At the end of the round, choose and sacrifice any duplicate creatures on the Field of
Play.
Continue these
steps until
you
reach the seventh and final seal, ending the turn.

Updated Siege Rules: The Dual Front
Clearing the Way: To attack an enemy Champion, you must first clear the Battlefield
position directly in front of you.
Lane Assistance: If Player A clears their lane, but their teammate Player B is still
blocked by a card, Player A can initiate a Siege on the Champion.
If both teammates clear their respective positions, they both engage the Champion.
In this case, their Power is not combined; instead, the Champion must defend against
two separate battles. If either teammate wins, the Champion is defeated.
Ascension Choice: When a Champion is defeated and the Seal is claimed, the winning
team decides which of their two victorious creatures will Ascend to become the new
Champion. The other creature remains on the Battlefield to guard the lane.

The Final Decree
Endless Seven is an infinite, unending war. The triumph of the Purified
offers
no lasting peace, just as the reign of the Corrupted provides no final
end; instead, each outcome serves as a cosmic reset for the next. Whether
the Light secures the seals or the Dark shatters them, the cycle is destined
to repeat, the fate of existence forever locked in a boundless loop of rebirth
and ruin.
